The Gaffer
Everything needed to rebuild the app's surfaces, controls and widgets.
One product, one device: a portrait phone at 390×844. Warm paper, ink text, green for action, red for forced, amber for caution. Figures are always mono. Nothing decorative earns space — every colour and every border in here carries meaning.

Forced is not confidence
Forced is a property of the call — an unplayable starter — not a threshold on the percentage. A 95% captaincy pick is Certain, never Forced: you always have a choice about an armband.
Decision states
Per call, not per category: Pending review, Selected · Locked, Rejected. Swipe right to select, left to reject, up to defer. Rejecting is removing — there is no separate remove.

ANATOMY · TOP TO BOTTOM
Shell — 2px #CFC7B6, overflow hidden so the band bleeds to the corners, white body, and a shadow by context: 0 6px 18px rgba(92,82,62,.12) for a card in a column, 0 10px 30px rgba(92,82,62,.14) for a card that fills the screen, 0 -10px 40px rgba(35,33,29,.28) for a banded sheet. Radius follows scale: 11px for a row or call card, 14px in a column, 18px for a banded sheet, 20px for a hero.
Band header — the card's one saturated element, always the same fill: linear-gradient(135deg,#0A6E3D,#10985A). No card uses a flat green, and no card uses a red or amber band — state is carried by a disc or a chip inside the band, never by recolouring it.
Body — 12–14px padding, one paragraph of 12px prose at 1.5. Never a figure without its label.
Figure strip — optional, on #F7F4EE with a 1px #E9E4DA rule above: two or three mono figures, 8px cap over 12px value, 16px apart.
Reasoning block — optional: mascot cutout on the left, text with a 2px #BFDCCB left rule. Clamped to four lines.
Footer — optional action row on the card face, buttons per §03 (cream faces on white).
Rules: one band per card, never two saturated bands on a screen; the band always states what this card is plus its scope; sections are separated by 1px #E9E4DA rules, never by gaps; nothing inside a banded card scrolls except a designated table region.

Failure · titled band, red disc
Failure is not a red band. The band stays green; the disc goes #E03A4E and the scope line states what survived. The Gaffer never shouts — the card is still his.

06 · LAYOUT & RULES
Frame
390×844, portrait only, 34px corner radius. 44px status bar, then header, then content, then a 22px home strip. Side padding 12–16px; radius 11px for a row or call card, 14px in a column, 16px for a sheet, 18–20px for a hero; gaps of 6, 8, 10 and 14px.
Nothing scrolls as a whole
Screens are exactly one viewport. Only named regions scroll: the stat table (both axes), the evaluation table inside a detail card, the candidate picker, and the Overview column.
Voice
The Gaffer speaks plainly and takes a position: one sentence of advice, one of reason. No hedging, no exclamation, no emoji. Real Premier League names and real figures — never lorem or placeholder data.
Empty and failure states
Every failure keeps the squad intact and says so. A cleared category shows what you decided and lets you tap any decision to reopen it. A refresh is always confirmed and always reports a diff.
